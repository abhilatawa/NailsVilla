Executive Summary

What we built, in one paragraph

Nails Villa is a real, working website and appointment-booking system for an independent nail-art business. A visitor can browse services, see real prices and durations, book an appointment in under a minute, and receive an instant confirmation — all without a phone call. Behind the scenes, the system is built the way a bank or airline booking system is built: it is technically impossible for two customers to be double-booked into the same time slot, even if they try at the exact same moment.

Where things stand today: the customer-facing website and the full booking engine are complete and have been tested end-to-end, including under real concurrent load. The owner's admin control panel (managing services, hours, and appointments from a dashboard) is the next phase of work.
The Opportunity

Why this matters for a small business

Independent, appointment-based businesses like a personal nail studio typically lose potential customers in a few predictable ways: no way to book outside of a phone call during business hours, no visible pricing or availability, and no professional web presence next to larger competitors. Every one of those is a lost booking.

Problem

Booking friction

Customers can only book by calling during open hours, and a missed call is often a lost customer.

Solution

24/7 self-serve booking

Customers see real availability and book instantly, any time of day, from any device.

Problem

No online presence

No way for a new customer to discover services, pricing, or the quality of the work.

Solution

A premium, mobile-first website

A clean, professional brand presence built around converting visitors into booked appointments.

Key Features

What the platform actually does

Real-time availability

Booking times shown to customers are calculated live from actual business hours, existing appointments, and blocked-off time — never a static calendar.

Double-booking protection

The system is built so that two customers can never be booked into the same slot, even if they submit at the exact same second — verified with an automated stress test.

Flexible pricing per service

Services can be priced as a fixed amount, a "starting from" price, or a range — matching how nail art is actually priced in practice.

Guest or account booking

Customers can book instantly as a guest, or create an account to manage upcoming and past appointments.

Built-in gallery & reviews

A photo gallery and customer review system are fully built and ready — populated as soon as the owner adds real photos and reviews start coming in.

Mobile-first design

Designed to be booked comfortably with one hand on a phone, since most visitors to a personal beauty business arrive on mobile.

Technology & Security

Built on a production-grade foundation

This isn't a template or a page builder — it's custom software, built with the same categories of technology used by established consumer booking platforms, chosen specifically so the business can grow into it rather than outgrow it.

  • Frontend (what customers see): a modern, fast web application (React) that works identically well on a phone, tablet, or desktop.
  • Backend (the business logic): a Java/Spring Boot application — an enterprise-grade platform used by banks, airlines, and large-scale e-commerce systems — handling every booking rule and security check.
  • Database: PostgreSQL, an industry-standard, highly reliable database used to store every service, appointment, and customer record safely.
  • Security: passwords are never stored in readable form (industry-standard one-way encryption), login sessions use secure, time-limited tokens, and every sensitive action is checked on the server — never trusted from the browser alone.
  • Portability: the entire system runs in standardized containers (Docker), meaning it can be deployed to any modern cloud provider without being locked into one vendor.
  • Quality assurance: the booking engine has automated tests that run the exact double-booking scenario described above and confirm it can never happen.
